# 🎨 Creating Custom Popups

Build your own popup components with full control over UI and behavior.

While the [native popups](/native-popups/) cover most use cases, you may need to create a custom component for:

- Highly specialized UI/UX that doesn't fit a standard modal.
- Complex internal state management.
- Re-using a specific popup logic throughout your app.

### The Process

1.  [Basic Structure & Props](/custom-popups/basic-structure): Understand the required props injected by `ntPopups`.
2.  [Styling System](/custom-popups/styling-system): Learn to use the provided CSS classes and attributes to maintain theme consistency.
3.  [Complete Example](/custom-popups/complete-example): See a full component example.
4.  [Register and Use](/custom-popups/register-and-use): Register your component in the `NtPopupProvider` and call it with `openPopup`.
